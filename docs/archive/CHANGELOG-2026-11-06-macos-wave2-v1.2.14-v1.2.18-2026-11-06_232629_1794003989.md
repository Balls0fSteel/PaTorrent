## 2026-11-06_160049_1793977249: Version 1.2.18: macOS releases switch to the native app (slice K)

- The release workflow's macOS job builds `rookparcel-mac` for arm64 and x86_64 (`MACOSX_DEPLOYMENT_TARGET=11.0`), joins them with `lipo` (checked universal), bundles with `macos_bundle.py native`, signs ad hoc, starts the app on the runner (`--version` natively and under Rosetta, a 20 s GUI start with a screenshot and `ROOKPARCEL_SNAPSHOTS`), and makes one `Rookparcel-<version>-macos.dmg` (verified, the signature checked inside); the nox tarballs stay. CI and gate 8 no longer install Homebrew GTK; gate 8 checks the native app and its `mac` folders (its stale `patorrent-gtk` greps are gone). D23 is superseded on macOS (DECISIONS); the Flatpak and Linux GTK are unchanged.
- The GTK crate loses its macOS code (`macos.rs`, `tray_macos.rs`, `HANDLES_OPEN`, the URL handler, the macOS paths, the translocation notice) and builds a stub on macOS; `macos_bundle.py` keeps `native` and `check` (the GTK `app`/`deps` commands and `macos_launcher.c` are gone), and `Info.plist` gains `NSAppleEventsUsageDescription` for Auto Shutdown's Apple events. The site (a new `dmg` kind; old `-arm64.dmg` releases still listed), README, wiki (install, files, ⌘ shortcuts, tray, Auto Shutdown) and RELEASING.md describe one dmg for every Mac on macOS 11.
- Checked: workspace clippy on Windows, the Mac and GTK crates for the Apple target, GTK linted for Linux with a stand-in pkg-config, fmt, the Mac and frontend tests, the bundler's and the site's tests, the site built `--strict`. **Untested until they run:** the release, CI and gate 8 workflows (watch the first run's macOS job: the two-target build, `lipo`, the start step, the dmg). The site's text goes live with the next push to `main`: push it together with the first native release, whose draft dmg is to be tried on a real Mac first.
